HAPPY BIRTHDAY WEBSITE 🌻
=========================

HOW TO USE
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Put your files inside the "assets" folder with EXACTLY these names:
     assets/video-singing.mp4   -> the video of you singing
     assets/video-edits.mp4     -> her edits
     assets/song.mp3            -> the background song you choose
   (Different names? Just change them in config.js.)
3. Open config.js in Notepad / any text editor and change the text:
   her nickname, the love letter, the 21 reasons, video titles, etc.
4. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser. That's it.

TIPS
- Music only starts after she taps "Open it", which is how browsers allow sound.
- While a video plays, the background song pauses and then resumes.
- On phones, .mp4 (H.264) and .mp3 work everywhere. Keep videos under ~50 MB each
  if you plan to host the site online.
- To send it to her: upload the whole folder to a free host like Netlify Drop
  (app.netlify.com/drop) or GitHub Pages, and share the link.
  Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so the first open needs internet.
